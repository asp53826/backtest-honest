=== the cost side: purging is not free ===

  Dropped training rows against label horizon, five folds.

   horizon  purged only  +2% embargo  +5% embargo
  -----------------------------------------------
         1        0.2%         2.2%         5.2%
         5        0.8%         2.8%         5.8%
        20        3.3%         5.3%         8.3%
        50        8.3%        10.3%        13.3%
       100       16.7%        18.7%        21.7%
       200       33.3%        35.3%        37.9%

  At a 200-bar horizon on 1,200 bars, purging removes most of the
  training set — which is the honest answer rather than a failure. A
  label reaching that far forward genuinely does contaminate almost
  everything adjacent to it, and a backtest that keeps those rows is
  not training on more data, it is training on the test set.
